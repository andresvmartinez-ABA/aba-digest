AI-Assisted Intervention Program for Enhancing Social and Communication Skills in Autism Spectrum Disorder

Ali Alhajri, Balaqis, Bader Al-Ofi, Marwa, Ahmed Al-Abri, Maryam et al.  ·  Journal of Physics: Conference Series  ·  2026
85/100This article directly matches the profile's interest in AI-assisted communication tools, technology-based interventions for children with autism, and social/communication outcomes in school-age populations, featuring practical applications rather than theoretical approaches.
Summary

This study aimed to develop and evaluate an AI-assisted intervention program designed to enhance social and communication skills in children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). The researchers created an integrated system consisting of a mobile application (Ezaf) featuring interactive emotion and social games plus a communication hub, and a web-based platform that uses facial recognition and machine learning for therapists and researchers. The system was developed using an Agile methodology with continuous feedback from professionals and caregivers, incorporating technologies like Flutter, Unity, and Firebase for secure data management and real-time progress tracking. Evaluation results indicated that the system demonstrated usability, effectiveness, and potential as a practical technology-driven support tool for children with ASD and their support teams.

Abstract

Abstract Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) often face challenges in communication, emotional expression, and social interaction, which can hinder their development and independence. Addressing these challenges requires personalized, accessible, and engaging interventions that support both children and caregivers. This project introduces an AI-Assisted Intervention Program designed to enhance the social and communication skills of children with ASD through two integrated platforms: a mobile application and a web-based system. The mobile app, Ezaf, developed using Flutter and Unity, features interactive emotion and social games, a Communication Hub for non-verbal expression, and a real-time dashboard that tracks progress metrics such as accuracy and response time. Firebase is used for secure authentication, data storage, and real-time updates. In parallel, the web platform supports therapists and researchers by leveraging facial recognition and machine learning to analyze children‘s visual data, aiding in early detection and personalized intervention planning. The system was developed following an Agile methodology, with continuous feedback from professionals and caregivers. Evaluation results confirm the system’s usability, effectiveness, and potential to serve as a practical, technology-driven support tool for children with ASD and those working to support them.
