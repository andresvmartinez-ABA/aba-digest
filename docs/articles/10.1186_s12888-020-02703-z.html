Examining the autistic traits in children and adolescents diagnosed with attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder and their parents.

Okyar, Esra, Görker, Işık  ·  BMC psychiatry  ·  2020
ASD + ADHD
75/100This article directly examines the comorbidity between ADHD and autism in children and adolescents, which aligns well with the profile's interest in ADHD-related reinforcement contingencies including comorbidity with ASD and focuses on the preferred child/youth population rather than adults.
Summary

This study examined the prevalence of autistic traits in children diagnosed with ADHD and their parents, as well as parental risk factors that may increase autistic traits in children. The researchers compared 66 children with ADHD and 33 control children without ADHD, along with their parents, using standardized screening tools including the Autism Behavior Checklist for children and the Autism Spectrum Quotient for parents. The study found that children diagnosed with ADHD exhibited significantly more autistic traits than the control group, with higher rates observed in boys and those with comorbid Oppositional Defiant Disorder. While parents of ADHD-diagnosed children showed more ADHD symptoms, they did not differ from control group parents in autism symptoms; however, both maternal and paternal ADHD symptoms were predictive of autism symptoms in their children. The research also identified maternal smoking during pregnancy as associated with increased autistic traits in children.
