This Week

What a fantastic week for research! We've got a strong showing from our colleagues at FTF Behavioral Consulting — Wang, Lin, Xu, Guangjun, Li, and Dan really made their mark with 17 papers this week. The dual diagnosis work on ASD and ADHD continues to heat up, and there's some compelling stuff on preference and functional communication training that caught my eye. Don't miss the systematic review on speech-generating devices and the fascinating AI piece on tailoring personally relevant interventions — both are real standouts. Here's what came in this week.
