This Week

Hey there — this week's digest leans heavy into tech, and it's a good one. We've got a solid cluster on PECS and speech-generating devices, a nice companion set on AAC more broadly, and a handful of pieces looking at how technology is shaping ABA practice day to day. It's the kind of lineup that's great if you've been meaning to brush up on device-based communication supports or just want to see where the field is heading tech-wise. Nothing flashy, just a lot of practical, well-timed research for anyone juggling AAC caseloads or thinking about tools for their practice. Here's what came in this week.
