This Week

Another week packed with fascinating research, and the FTF Behavioral Consulting team really dominated the landscape with Beaulieu, Whelan, Hanley, Landa, Metras, and Slaton contributing to 16 papers across multiple areas. We're seeing some compelling work on delay to reinforcement tolerance treatments that's really pushing the field forward, plus solid contributions in automatically reinforced self-injurious behavior interventions. Don't miss the standout pieces on "Telehealth Versus In-Person Caregiver-Mediated Behavioral Treatment" and "Functional analysis: what have we learned in 85 years?" — both are generating some serious buzz. Here's what came in this week.
