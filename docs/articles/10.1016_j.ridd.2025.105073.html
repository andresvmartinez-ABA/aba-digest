Comorbidity aggregation models in children and adolescents with ADHD and direct and moderator effects of familial clinical history and psychosocial factors.

Melegari, M G, Donfrancesco, R, Sacco, R et al.  ·  Research in developmental disabilities  ·  2025
ASD + ADHD
75/100This article directly addresses ADHD comorbidity patterns in children and adolescents, which aligns well with the profile's interest in ADHD-related reinforcement contingencies, comorbidity with ASD, and the specified focus on preschool and school-age populations rather than adults.
Summary

This study aimed to identify patterns of comorbid mental disorders in children and adolescents with ADHD and examine how family psychiatric history and psychosocial factors influence these comorbidity patterns. The researchers used Principal Component Analysis on data from 1,086 ADHD probands, along with regression and moderator analyses to assess the effects of first-degree relatives' mental disorders and socio-environmental factors. The analysis identified four distinct comorbidity models: anxiety-depressive-conduct disorders, intellectual-language-motor disorders, tic-Tourette/autism versus bipolar disorder, and opposition-conduct versus learning disabilities. Key findings showed that sibling ADHD history and socio-environmental factors affected multiple comorbidity patterns, while first-degree relatives' psychiatric disorders directly influenced anxiety-depressive-conduct comorbidities, and maternal ADHD moderated by sibling ADHD rates affected tic-related comorbidity profiles.
