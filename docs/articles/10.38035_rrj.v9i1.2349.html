Summary

# Summary: AI Integration for Autism Spectrum Disorder This mini review synthesized findings from 55 studies (2020–2024) to explore how Artificial Intelligence can advance diagnosis, intervention, and personalized care for individuals with Autism Spectrum Disorder. The review examined AI methodologies including Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), ResNet 18, and hybrid models such as GoogleNet SVM, as well as multimodal approaches integrating visual, audio, and textual data alongside virtual reality, telehealth, and IoT-based monitoring tools. The studies reported that CNN and hybrid AI models demonstrated high accuracy in ASD diagnosis, and multimodal AI approaches enhanced contextual understanding, while AI-supported interventions showed promise across virtual reality, telehealth, and remote monitoring platforms. For BCBAs and autism practitioners, these findings suggest potential applications for AI-assisted diagnostic support and telehealth-delivered interventions; however, significant implementation considerations include addressing access inequities, ethical concerns, and the current research gap regarding adult ASD populations. The review underscores the need for inclusive and ethically-designed AI innovations to ensure equitable benefit across diverse ASD populations.

Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) presents transformative possibilities for Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) diagnosis and intervention. This mini review synthesizes findings from a scoping review of 55 studies (2020–2024), exploring AI’s role in early detection, multimodal interventions, and personalized care. While Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), ResNet 18, and hybrid models such as GoogleNet SVM show high accuracy in diagnosing ASD, novel approaches integrating visual, audio, and textual data enhance contextual understanding. AI tools also support intervention through virtual reality, telehealth, and IoT based monitoring. Nevertheless, challenges such as access inequities, ethical concerns, and research gaps on adult ASD populations persist. This review highlights the importance of inclusive, ethical innovation in AI powered ASD care.
