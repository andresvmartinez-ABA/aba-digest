# Summary: Effects of Aided Augmented Input on Autism Spectrum Disorder This scoping review examined the existing literature on aided augmented input—an intervention in which communication partners use visual symbols to support expressive communication and comprehension—in children and adolescents with ASD who experience complex communication needs. The researchers analyzed 17 studies from Web of Science, Scopus, and PubMed using PRISMA-ScR guidelines, examining AAC resources, implementation contexts, and reported outcomes. The review found that aided augmented input was associated with benefits across communication, social skills, and behavior; however, the abstract does not specify effect sizes or quantitative outcome measures. BCBAs and autism practitioners should recognize aided augmented input as a potentially beneficial intervention strategy for clients with ASD and complex communication needs, while acknowledging that the current evidence base would benefit from additional rigorous research to establish specific efficacy parameters and implementation guidelines.

Abstract

People diagnosed with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) often experience complex communication needs and developmental challenges. As a result, various intervention strategies incorporating augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) resources and supports are utilised. One notable approach is aided augmented input, an intervention that involves using visual symbols provided by a communication partner to support both expressive communication and comprehension. This scoping review aims to examine the existing literature on this intervention in children and adolescents with ASD, following PRISMA-ScR guidelines. 17 studies from databases such as Web of Science, Scopus, and PubMed were analysed, focusing on AAC resources, implementation contexts, and key findings. Results suggest that aided augmented input benefits users with ASD by enhancing communication, social skills, and behaviour. However, further research is needed to better understand its effects.
