Summary

# Summary: AI-Enabled Mobile Support for Autism Caregivers This scoping review examined the integration of artificial intelligence technologies—including machine learning prediction models and large language models (LLMs)—into mobile platforms designed to support autism caregivers managing home-based interventions. The methodology involved a PRISMA-compliant scoping review of 33 studies published between 2019 and 2026, analyzing how AI-enabled systems track behavioral data (social communication, repetitive behaviors, sensory responses) and generate caregiver support through predictive analytics and LLM-based recommendations. Key findings revealed that while 72.8% of AI-driven diagnostic studies demonstrate high clinical relevance, only 41.0% provide sufficient implementation detail for reproducibility; additionally, four pilot programs from Indonesia, Italy, and India demonstrated individual components (progress recording, stress sensing, intervention delivery) but none integrated predictive analytics or LLM assistance into a unified validated platform. The authors propose an integrated AI-Sory Talk framework combining progress monitoring, machine learning-based trajectory prediction, and LLM-generated personalized intervention suggestions to address current implementation gaps. For BCBAs and autism practitioners, this framework highlights the potential of integrated AI-mobile platforms to extend professional support capacity during provider shortages, though current evidence remains limited to isolated pilot studies, and implementation in clinical practice will require validated platforms, clinician oversight protocols, and demonstrated effectiveness in improving social, communication, and sensory outcomes.

Abstract

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a complex neurodevelopmental condition affecting approximately 1 in 36 children, marked by persistent deficits in social communication and repetitive behaviors. A shortage of specialized professionals has produced long wait times, motivating caregivers to mediated home support. This document suggests a conceptual structure (one that was established through a PRISMA-SCR compliant scoping review of 33 studies) for AI assisted mobile support in autism caregiving (2019 to 2026). Progress analytics log home activity and behavioral data, sometimes fused with movement, heart rate, speech, or video signals, to track social, communication, and sensory changes, while machine learning based predictive analytics forecast engagement and stagnation, and LLM based assistance turns these insights into personalized caregiver suggestions. However, studies commonly treat these functions separately, with limited evidence of integration in a validated home platform. Four independently developed mobile pilots from Indonesia, Italy, and India demonstrate caregiver facing progress recording, stress sensing, and personalized intervention delivery, one combining sensing, AI based classification, and caregiver alerting in a single low cost prototype, yet none includes predictive analytics or LLM based assistance. A translational paradox further constrains the field: 72.8% of AI driven diagnostic studies show high clinical relevance, yet only 41.0% report sufficient detail for independent reproduction. Building on these gaps, we propose a conceptual integrated mobile support framework combining progress monitoring and tracking analytics, machine learning based predictive analytics of developmental trajectories, and an LLM based chatbot for personalized activity and intervention suggestions, structured around a caregiver centered Recommend, Perform, Record, Analyze, Predict, Adapt, Support framework with clinician in the loop oversight, to improve social, communication, and sensory outcomes for children with ASD and advance SDG 3, Good Health and Well Being.
