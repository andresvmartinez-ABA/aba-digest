Summary

# Summary: Technology-Based Interventions for Literacy in ASD This systematic review examined the effectiveness of technology-based interventions in promoting literacy skills and learning engagement among children with autism spectrum disorder. The methodology involved a systematic review process that synthesized existing empirical studies evaluating various technology-based literacy interventions designed for this population. The review identified that technology-based interventions demonstrated positive effects on literacy outcomes, though specific effect sizes and comparative efficacy data varied across included studies depending on intervention type, participant characteristics, and outcome measures employed. For BCBAs and autism practitioners, these findings suggest that technology-based tools may serve as supplementary or primary intervention components for addressing literacy deficits; however, practitioners should evaluate individual student responsiveness and ensure interventions are integrated within comprehensive, behavior-analytic treatment plans rather than implemented in isolation.
