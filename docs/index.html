This Week

Tech is dominating the conversation this week, with a hefty batch of articles diving into how we're integrating technology into ABA practice more broadly, alongside some nice focused pieces on tech-supported PECS and SGD implementation. There's also a solid contribution on visual schedules and transitions, which pairs well with the AAC/SGD piece rounding things out. If you've been curious about where tech and communication supports are headed in our field right now, this is a good week to catch up. The through-line across these pieces is practical: how do we actually use these tools well, not just adopt them because they're shiny. Here's what came in this week.
