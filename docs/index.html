This Week

Hey there — this week's digest leans heavily into AAC and SGDs, with a nice cluster of pieces exploring how technology is reshaping everyday ABA practice. We've also got a couple of studies bridging tech with PECS and speech-generating devices, which pairs nicely with the broader AAC conversation happening across the issue. Rounding things out is a single but compelling look at stereotypy as reinforcement alongside functional living escape considerations — worth a close read if that's in your wheelhouse. No FTF authors in this batch, but the technology-focused pieces are generating some real buzz for good reason. Here's what came in this week.
