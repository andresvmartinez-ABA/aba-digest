This Week

Hey there — this week's digest leans heavily into the tech side of our field, with a nice cluster of pieces on technology in ABA alongside some thoughtful work on AAC and speech-generating devices. There's a lot here for anyone tracking how PECS and SGD tools are evolving alongside digital supports, plus a compassionate care piece that's a great reminder of the 'why' behind all the systems we build. No FTF authors in this batch, but the range of perspectives still makes for a solid, well-rounded read. Whether you're more interested in the hardware or the heart of practice, there's something worth bookmarking. Here's what came in this week.
