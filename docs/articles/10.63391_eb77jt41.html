# Summary: Assistive Technologies and Autism Spectrum Disorder for Educational Inclusion This bibliographic review examined how assistive technologies (AT) can support educational inclusion and skill development in students with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). The research employed a literature review methodology to synthesize information on AT applications in inclusive educational settings, focusing on both conceptual frameworks and practical implementations. The article reports that various forms of AT have demonstrated contributions to social, academic, and cognitive skill development in students with ASD, and that these technologies can help overcome communication, socialization, and learning barriers in school environments. For BCBA practitioners and educators, this review underscores the potential value of integrating accessible and equitable assistive technologies into educational programs to support inclusion, though practitioners should note that the article identifies both benefits and challenges in classroom technology implementation. This resource provides an overview of technology options for educators working with students with ASD, emphasizing the role of AT in promoting accessible and high-quality inclusive education.

Abstract

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a condition that significantly impacts social interaction, communication, and behavior in children and adults. The use of Assistive Technologies (AT) in the educational context has proven to be a crucial tool in promoting the inclusion of students with ASD, enabling them to overcome barriers in communication, socialization, and learning. This article discusses various forms of assistive technologies applied to inclusive education, highlighting their contribution to the development of social, academic, and cognitive skills. The research is bibliographic in nature, addressing both the concept of AT and its applicability to students with ASD, as well as the benefits and challenges in using these technologies in the school environment. The goal is to present an overview of technologies that can be used to facilitate the inclusion of these students, emphasizing the importance of accessible, equitable, and high-quality education.
