This Week

Happy to see Fahmie well represented this week with three papers in the mix — always worth a close read. The field continues its steady march toward practical application, with a nice cluster of pieces on technology in ABA alongside a couple of thoughtful entries on social validity and another pair digging into visual schedules and transitions. It's a good week for anyone thinking about how our interventions actually land with the people we serve, not just whether they work on paper. There's a nice balance here between tech-forward tools and the more foundational question of whether our clients and caregivers actually value what we're doing. Ten articles total, and honestly a solid spread across topics that tend to spark good team discussions. Here's what came in this week.
