This Week

Hey there — this week's digest leans heavily into the tech side of our field, with six pieces on technology in ABA and a nice cluster of three on AAC and speech-generating devices rounding things out. It's a good mix if you've been trying to keep up with how digital tools and communication supports are evolving alongside our practice. Whether you're curious about app-based data collection, telehealth adaptations, or how SGDs are being integrated into intervention plans, there's something here worth bookmarking. No FTF spotlights this time, but the breadth across these nine articles makes for a solid snapshot of where the field is heading. Here's what came in this week.
