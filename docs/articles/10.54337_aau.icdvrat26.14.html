Protocol for a Randomised Controlled Trial and Feasibility Evaluation of a Large Language Model- Based Digital Social Interaction Programme to Improve the Social Communication Skills of Autistic Children in China

Vicky Zhao, W. Farr  ·  Proceedings of the International Conference on Disability, Virtual Reality &amp; Associated Technologies  ·  2026
TECHNOLOGY IN ABA

Summary

# Research Summary This study aims to evaluate the effectiveness of a large language model-based digital social interaction programme in improving social communication skills among autistic children aged 3-8 years in China, where access to long-term social communication intervention is limited. The researchers will conduct a 12-week randomized controlled trial comparing the digital intervention (social story videos combined with LLM-driven conversational practice) plus standard care against standard care alone. Primary outcomes will be measured using the Vineland-3 Socialization score, with secondary measures including the Social Responsiveness Scale (SRS) and Autism Behavior Checklist (ABC). This research may provide evidence supporting the feasibility and effectiveness of AI-supported home-based social communication interventions as an accessible treatment option for young autistic children, potentially addressing significant service gaps in regions with limited intervention availability. The study protocol does not yet report outcome results, as it describes a planned trial rather than completed data.

Abstract

In China, 0.7% of school‑aged children have autism. Access to long‑term social communication intervention remains limited. We evaluate a large-language-model-based digital social interaction programme designed for autistic children in China aged between 3-8 years old. The intervention integrates social story videos with real-time LLM-driven conversational practice to enhance social communication skills. Randomized controlled trial will compare the digital intervention plus standard care, with standard care alone over 12-weeks. Primary outcomes: Vineland-3 Socialization score. Secondary measure: Social Responsiveness Scale (SRS) and Autism Behavior Checklist (ABC). We aim to provide evidence for AI-supported home-based social communication interventions for young children with autism.
