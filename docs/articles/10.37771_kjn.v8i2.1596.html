Summary

# Summary: Visual Schedule-Based Bedtime Routine for Sleep Disturbances in Children with Autism This study aimed to evaluate the effectiveness of a visual schedule-based bedtime routine in reducing sleep disturbances in children with autism spectrum disorder. The researchers conducted a quasi-experimental study with a non-randomized control group pretest-posttest design, enrolling 30 children with autism (ages 6–18 years) divided into experimental and control groups (n=15 each), with the intervention implemented daily for one hour before bedtime over two weeks. Post-intervention Children's Sleep Habits Questionnaire (CSHQ) scores showed a statistically significant difference between groups (experimental group mean = 45.07; control group mean = 48.80; p = 0.00), indicating that the visual schedule-based bedtime routine effectively reduced sleep disturbances. The visual schedule-based bedtime routine represents a practical, evidence-supported intervention that BCBAs and autism practitioners can implement to address common sleep problems and reduce associated caregiver stress and child emotional dysregulation.

Abstract

Sleep disturbances are a common challenge for children with autism, often leading to caregiver stress, emotional dysregulation, and developmental delays. This study aims to evaluate the effect of a visual schedule-based bedtime routine on reducing sleep disturbances in children with autism. Research Methods: A quasi-experimental study with a non-randomized control group pretest posttest design was conducted. Thirty children with autism (aged 6–18 years) were selected through purposive sampling and divided into experimental and control groups (n=15 each). The intervention consisted of a visual schedule-based bedtime routine implemented daily for two weeks, starting one hour before bedtime. Data were collected using the Indonesian version of the Children's Sleep Habits Questionnaire (CSHQ), which demonstrated reliability (Cronbach Alpha = 0.80). Statistical analysis was performed using the Independent Sample T-test. The findings showed a significant difference in post intervention CSHQ scores, with a mean of 45.07 in the experimental group and 48.80 in the control group (p = 0.00; p < 0.05). This indicates that the implementation of a visual schedule-based bedtime routine effectively reduces sleep disturbances by increasing bedtime comfort and supporting the physiological and psychological deactivation process necessary for sleep. Gangguan tidur merupakan salah satu permasalahan yang sering terjadi pada anak autisme. Dampak dari gangguan tidur ini dapat dirasakan oleh keluarga sebagai primary care, seperti lelah dan stres, maupun anak secara langsung seperti gangguan regulasi emosi dan keterlambatan perkembangan. Salah satu intervensi untuk mengatasi kondisi gangguan tidur yaitu penggunaan bedtime routine berbasis visual schedule. Penelitian ini bertujuan untuk mengidentifikasi pengaruh penggunaan bedtime routine berbasis visual schedule terhadap gangguan tidur anak autisme. Metode penelitian kuantitatif dengan desain penelitian quasi-experiment menggunakan non-random control group pretest-posttest. Responden pada penelitian ini ditentukan melalui purposive sampling yang terdiri dari 30 anak autisme usia 6–18 tahun yang dibagi dalam kelompok eksperimen dan kontrol, masing-masing 15 anak. Intervensi berupa pelaksanaan bedtime routine berbasis visual schedule dilakukan setiap hari satu jam sebelum tidur selama 2 minggu. Instrumen penelitian yang digunakan ialah Children's Sleep Habit Questionnaire (CSHQ) versi Bahasa Indonesia yang telah valid dan reliabel dengan nilai Cronbach Alpha 0,80. Analisis data yang digunakan ialah analisis parametrik yaitu Independent Sample T-test. Hasil penelitian ini menunjukkan adanya penurunan nilai CSHQ yang signifikan dengan rata-rata kelompok eksperimen 45,07 dan kelompok kontrol 48,80 dengan p-value 0,00 (p < 0,05). Maka, hasil penelitian penerapan intervensi bedtime routine berbasis visual schedule dapat menurunkan gangguan tidur dengan meningkatkan kenyamanan menjelang tidur pada anak autisme. Hal ini merupakan bagian dari proses deaktivasi fisiologis dan psikologis yang memfasilitasi tidur.
