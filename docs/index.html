This Week

Hey there — this week's digest leans heavy into technology, with five articles digging into how tech is reshaping ABA practice and two more zooming in specifically on PECS and speech-generating devices. There's also a nice pairing of a social validity piece and a visual schedules/transitions study that round things out nicely if you're thinking about practical, classroom-ready applications. It's a good week if you've been curious about where the field is headed on the tech front, but the social validity piece is a worthwhile reminder that stakeholder buy-in still matters just as much as the tools themselves. Nothing here feels like filler — it's a tight, focused set. Here's what came in this week.
