Field Pulse

AI & Technology in ABA — Recent Advances

AI-Assisted Speech & Language Therapy

Recent contributions to AI-assisted speech and language therapy have focused heavily on autism-related communication support. Wang et al. offer a narrative review reframing AI-supportive systems for neurodivergent children around design logic rather than diagnostic labels, noting that expressive communication difficulties observed across five studies were not exclusive to ASD, even though much of the underlying language-support evidence originates from ASD-focused augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) research showing improvements in children with ASD. Complementing this systemic perspective, Enkhjargal, Lalwani, and Salam present AdaptED Stories, an AI-assisted social story intervention designed as an AAC tool for special education, aimed at supporting in-the-moment communication activities for children with autism spectrum disorder.
