Preliminary Development and Testing of the Risk Assessment Checklist for Self-Injury in Autism-Medical (RACSA-M).

Alberts, Lisa B, Kettering, Tracy L  ·  Journal of doctoral nursing practice  ·  2022
SIB PREDICTORS
75/100This article directly addresses self-injurious behavior in children with autism and presents a behavioral assessment tool, which aligns well with the profile's interests in SIB, behavioral assessment methodology, and the medical underpinnings of self-injury, though it focuses more on medical risk factors than behavioral interventions.
Summary

This study aimed to develop and preliminarily validate the Risk Assessment Checklist for Self-Injury in Autism-Medical (RACSA-M), a standardized assessment tool for evaluating medical risk factors associated with self-injurious behavior in children with autism spectrum disorder. The researchers created the checklist based on a literature review of medical conditions linked to SIB and tested it for physical, behavioral, and diagnostic evaluation of non-verbal children with autism and SIB in residential settings. The study established preliminary content validity, criterion-related validity, and interobserver agreement for the RACSA-M. The findings suggest that the RACSA-M shows promise as an instrument for assessing underlying medical issues in non-verbal children with ASD who engage in self-injurious behavior.
