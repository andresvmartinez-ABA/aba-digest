Summary

# Summary: Risk-Controlled Multimodal Emotion Coaching for Autism Support The primary objective was to develop and validate a risk-controlled multimodal emotion coaching (RC-MEC) system that provides personalized and safe affective learning for individuals with autism by addressing limitations in existing digital interventions for social communication and emotion recognition. The methodology employed a multimodal framework combining vision and speech using self-supervised learning (SSL) encoders, with a risk-control module driven by conformal prediction to dynamically regulate intervention delivery and ensure safety; validation occurred on the FER-2013 facial emotion and RAVDESS speech datasets. Key results demonstrated 90.1% coverage at the target risk level (α = 0.1) and 92.2% singleton accuracy when the system operated within its confidence threshold, indicating reliable emotion recognition with controlled error tolerance. The risk-aware design offers BCBAs and autism practitioners a potentially safer tool for emotion coaching interventions by limiting recommendations to high-confidence predictions and preventing unreliable feedback delivery. This framework represents an advancement toward more user-centered and responsible assistive technologies for autism support, though further real-world validation with individuals with autism would be necessary to establish clinical utility.

Abstract

Challenges in social communication and emotion recognition characterize autism spectrum disorder (ASD). However, many existing digital interventions often fail, lacking the multimodal, adaptive, and safety-conscious frameworks necessary for adequate real-world support. This paper introduces a risk-controlled multimodal emotion coaching (RC-MEC) system designed to provide personalized and safe affective learning for individuals with autism. Our framework combines vision and speech using self-supervised learning (SSL) to capture rich, contextualized representations. We validated RC-MEC on the FER-2013 facial emotion and RAVDESS speech datasets. At the core of RC-MEC is a risk-control module, driven by conformal prediction, which dynamically regulates the agent's action. Interventions are delivered only within a predefined low-risk confidence limit, achieving 90.1 % coverage at the target risk level or error tolerance$(\alpha)=0.1$and 92.2 % singleton accuracy when confident. The results confirm that the proposed multimodal and risk-aware system offers a safer, more effective, and reliable tool for emotion coaching in autism, thereby paving the way for more responsible and user-centered assistive technologies.
